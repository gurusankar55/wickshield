How the verdict is made

Four steps, no single judge

1

You file the exchange, coin, minute and liquidation price.

2

Every validator reads the 1-minute candles (±1 min) from Binance, OKX, MEXC, KuCoin and Coinbase, plus the mark price on Binance and OKX.

3

Past candles never change, so validators must agree on the exact numbers (strict_eq).

4

Plain code decides. ABNORMAL_WICK needs: price reached on your exchange, no other exchange reached it, and a gap of 0.5% or more.
